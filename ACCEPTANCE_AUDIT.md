**Manual staging smoke sequence for the authorized operator.**

1. Restore a backup to a separate staging PostgreSQL database. Run the documented migration using the migration role, serve with the restricted application role, verify TLS/proxy limits and test rollback to the backup.
2. Sign in with two staging tenant identities via PKCE. Submit a facility upload. Verify the other tenant cannot read its audit, photo or mission and that a viewer cannot approve.
3. With approved reuse permission, import a reviewed postal locality and independently review a consenting business service. Check pending, revoked and expired records, fee and quote validity, then approve through authorized staff. Retain actual handover and downstream evidence separately.
4. Have qualified staff register a calibrated meter. Compare timestamped readings to the instrument, confirm missing coverage is reported, then verify a maintenance comparison using comparable intervals. Record the uncertainty and occupancy conditions.
5. Keep provider transfer disabled until the operator validates privacy, consent, model settings and quotas. Confirm the offline path still works. Record manual results before any release decision.
