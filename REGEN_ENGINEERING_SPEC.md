## 5. P1: real agentic orchestration
Keep existing names. Use a small typed persistent state machine or LangGraph only if it reduces complexity. Select relevant agents based on goal and available evidence; skip irrelevant agents with reason. Tools are typed, allowlisted, validated and read-only by default. ## 6. P1: Waste-to-Wealth — real recovery agent
Inputs: free-text material (English/Tamil where feasible), quantity/unit, condition, contamination, use context, optional photo, PIN code, user preferred effort and pathway. Known taxonomy is a tool, not limit. For unknown material: retrieve source-backed material guidance where available, ask targeted clarification, validate structured classification, record uncertainty; never force a match, invent price or pretend all waste is recyclable. Unknown or potentially hazardous materials require manual review and appropriately authorized professional channels; no DIY processing advice. Evaluate sell, donate, internal reuse, industrial symbiosis, collective recovery, recycling and safe authorized disposal when necessary. Rank by eligibility, actual demand, net value (accepted quantity x quote minus fees/transport/prep), avoided purchases, effort, reliability and impact; weights explicit, safety mandatory. Present concise actionable options with evidence, assumptions, preparation effort, constraints, next step and verification date. Avoid generic craft ideas. No demand means no actionable donation or exchange match. No quote means price unavailable or dated indicative range with source; never fake earnings. Use agent replanning if rejected, capacity changes, no minimum quantity or no eligible provider. Keep a mission open for authorized adult/facility staff approval and evidence. No direct private contact or collection coordination for minors.
